Datum plane — side elevation, real altitudes

The layouts above are top-down because they mock the chrome. The datum plane is the one thing that only makes sense in elevation, so here it is drawn from the side with the real altitudes of the live contacts above. Click any aircraft to move the datum to it.

Elevation view · selected datum

Amber contacts sit within ±1000 ft of the datum — the RVSM vertical separation minimum. That is the whole point of the feature: co-altitude traffic announces itself instead of having to be measured by eye. Drop-lines run to the datum, not the ground, so their length reads directly as altitude difference. The plane is finite (50 nm default), so it stops where the comparison stops being meaningful.
